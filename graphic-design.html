FAQ

Common Design Questions

What types of graphics can you create?

Custom logos, business cards, social media graphics, website banners, and marketing materials. If you need something else, share the details in the inquiry and I’ll let you know whether it’s a fit.

How much does a custom logo cost?

Custom logo design is $400. Graphic and digital design projects start at $150 and are quoted by project.

Can you work with my existing brand colors and logo?

Yes. I can design from your current brand, or help establish a simple visual direction if you don’t have one yet - including a new logo if you need one.

Can I request a logo and other graphics together?

Yes. Mention both in the inquiry and I’ll quote the logo and any additional design work together so the scope stays clear.

How many revisions are included?

Revision rounds are confirmed in your quote so the scope stays clear. Share the project details and I’ll outline what’s included before work begins.

What file formats will I receive?

Deliverable file formats depend on the project and are confirmed in your quote before work begins.

How long does a design project take?

Timing depends on the type of design, the amount of content, and how quickly feedback comes back. I’ll share an estimated timeline with your quote.
