SEO for Small Businesses in Colorado

Trigden Studio sets up search for small businesses that already have a website and need Google to understand it. The work is on the page itself: the title, the structure, the words, and the speed. It is based in Denver and used by businesses across Colorado.

Start a project hello@trigdenstudio.com

Make the site match the search

A homepage that tries to be every service at once is hard for Google to place. This engagement gives each important page a specific title, a heading that says what the business does, and internal links so Google can find those pages.

It also covers the basics that keep a small site from being ignored: a sitemap, a clear description, and images and scripts that do not drag the page down. Rankings are not promised. The goal is a site Google can read and a customer can understand.

What’s included

  • On-page SEO setup
  • Keyword focus for the pages you already have
  • Meta titles and descriptions
  • Page speed improvements

Who this is for

  • A local business with a site that rarely shows up in Google Search Console.
  • A one-page site that needs separate pages for the services people actually search for.
  • A redesign that launched without titles, a sitemap, or a page for each offer.
