Privacy Policy - Trigden Studio

Last updated: January 2026

Trigden Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you visit or interact with our website.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you submit a form or contact us:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Any message or project details you provide

We may also collect basic technical data such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Pages visited

This data is used only for site performance and analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Communicate about projects or services
  • Improve our website and services
  • Maintain business records

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses third-party providers for hosting, email, form delivery, cookie consent, and optional analytics. These providers process information needed to deliver their services under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Google Analytics is subject to your analytics consent choice.

Cookies & Tracking

Trigden Studio uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website. CookieYes provides the cookie consent controls. Optional analytics are disabled by default and Google Analytics is loaded only after you consent to analytics cookies. We do not use advertising cookies or retargeting pixels.

Some essential technical data may still be processed to keep the site functioning and secure (for example, basic server logs, form delivery, and spam/security filtering through third-party service providers).

You can accept or reject optional analytics through the CookieYes banner and change your choice through its consent controls. Rejecting or withdrawing consent disables analytics storage; accepting again re-enables it. Essential site functions and contact forms remain available without analytics consent.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Your Rights

You may request:

  • Access to your data
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your data

To make a request, contact us through the website.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
