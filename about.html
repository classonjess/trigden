The Person Behind the Studio

Hi, I'm Jess.

I'm the founder and designer behind Trigden Studio. I create websites and digital design that help businesses show up online with clarity, polish, and personality.

My path started in full-stack web development and grew into design and marketing. That background lets me think about structure, visuals, and business goals together - without passing you between different people.

I founded Trigden Studio because I wanted a more personal way to work: honest conversation, thoughtful craft, and websites shaped around each client's needs instead of a one-size-fits-all process.

I work with business owners who value clear communication and want a partner who stays involved from early ideas through launch.
