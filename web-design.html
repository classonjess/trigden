FAQ

Common Questions

How much does a website cost?

Custom website design starts at $1,500 for up to five pages and the standard features listed above. Larger sites, e-commerce, and extra custom work are quoted separately. Final pricing depends on scope, timeline, and the number of pages needed.

How long does a website take to build?

Most projects take 2–4 weeks, depending on scope, how quickly content is available, and the feedback round.

Can I update my website myself?

Yes. WordPress sites are set up so you can update everyday content. If you’d rather not, Growth Care includes up to 30 minutes of minor content edits per month. See maintenance plans and eligibility.

Is hosting included?

Hosting, domains, and third-party subscriptions are separate unless they’re included in a written proposal. We can connect the domain and hosting you already have, or help you get set up.

Is SEO included?

Basic on-page SEO setup is part of the website package. Ongoing SEO and performance work is a separate service.

Do you offer website maintenance?

Yes. Maintenance is optional and available for qualifying websites after review. Visit Website Maintenance & Support for current packages, pricing, eligibility, and exclusions.
